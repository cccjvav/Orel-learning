## Course materials

The course is **Online and Reinforcement Learning (OReL), University of Copenhagen, 2026**. Source PDFs and the supplied course schedule are in `OReL Material/OReL Material/`, retrieved from `origin/main`. The duplicate ZIP archive was not copied. Lessons refer to PDF page numbers (counting from 1), because slide overlays can make printed slide numbers differ.
